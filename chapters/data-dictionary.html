Appendix B — Data dictionary

Every column of the vehicle table, vehicles_enhanced.parquet. The year columns (mileage_YYYY, legit_mileage_YYYY and pca_YYYY) are shown once each, with the range of their missing shares across the years. This table is generated from the pipeline’s data_dictionary.csv, so it always matches the version of the data the book describes. Missing is the share of the 93,352,241 vehicles with no value.
