31  Emissions

31.1 Overview

These columns describe the vehicle’s official emissions: its CO2 figure and the Euro emission standard it was approved to. Both are recorded by the DVLA, not DVSA, and both are filled from similar vehicles where the DVLA has no value. Chapter 4 explains what the figures mean and their limits.

  • co2 is the official CO2 figure in g/km, measured when the model was type approved. It is the DVLA value where the VES record describes this vehicle, or else the most common value among vehicles of the same make, model, birth year, fuel and engine size, and failing that of the same make, model, birth year and fuel. A recorded 0 g/km is kept only for vehicles with no engine; anywhere else it is cleared and filled from similar vehicles. co2_src and co2_check record what was done (methodology, section 5.6).
  • euro_status is the Euro standard as text (EURO 6 AP), standardised from the DVLA’s many spellings, backfilled from similar vehicles, and otherwise assumed to be the standard compulsory for new vehicles of that type when the vehicle was made (section 5.7). euro_standard is its number (1 to 6), and euro_status_src and euro_check record where it came from and whether it fits the vehicle’s age.
  • co2Emissions_DVLA, euroStatus_DVLA and realDrivingEmissions_DVLA are the values as supplied.
ImportantRecorded, filled and assumed values

Filled and assumed values are estimates, not records. Results that depend on CO2 or Euro standard should be checked using only the recorded values (co2_src = 'dvla', euro_status_src = 'dvla'): Chapter 17 shows how, and Chapter 14 explains why.

31.2 Coverage

CO2 has been recorded since vehicle tax began to depend on it, for cars registered from March 2001, so the DVLA figure is almost never present for vehicles first used before then (Figure 31.1). The backfill cannot help here, as similar vehicles of the same age have no figure either: co2 is present for only 9% of vehicles first used before 2000, against 93% of those first used in the 2010s. The DVLA figure dips again for the newest vehicles, not yet looked up in the VES (Chapter 36), and the backfill fills most of that gap.

The DVLA recorded a Euro status for only 15% of vehicles, almost all first used since 2018. The backfill and the assumed standard raise this to 91% with a standard (euro_standard), but most of the values are therefore not records. For cars, 59% of Euro statuses are assumed from the date. euro_status is never blank: where no standard applies it says why instead, pre_euro for vehicles made before the first standard of their class (in 1992 for cars), no_schedule for body types with no Euro schedule and no_date where the vehicle has no date to place it. euro_standard is blank for these.

realDrivingEmissions_DVLA, the DVLA’s Real Driving Emissions (RDE) field, is present only for some vehicles first used since 2019, and is not used by the pipeline.

31.3 Values

How the values of each column change over time, by year of first use, from 1990. Numeric columns and dates show the minimum, lower quartile (Q1), median, upper quartile (Q3) and maximum; other columns show the share of vehicles with each value, with the less common values grouped as All other values and missing values as Blank.

co2

CO2 emissions (g/km): DVLA (same vehicle only), else variant, else model_year_fuel backfill (see co2_src). A recorded 0 is kept only for Electric and Fuel Cells vehicles; for anything else it is impossible, so it is cleared before the backfill and the vehicle is filled from similar vehicles (see co2_check).

co2_src

Source of the matching harmonised column: dvsa or dvla (recorded), variant (same make/model/birth year/fuel/engine size) or model_year_fuel (same make/model/birth year/fuel).

co2_check

CO2 check: ok; zero_replaced (a recorded 0 g/km on a vehicle with an engine, backfilled from similar vehicles); zero_removed (the same, but no backfill value was available); missing (no CO2 recorded or backfilled).

co2Emissions_DVLA

As returned by the DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service.

euro_status

Euro status: DVLA with the spelling standardised (‘EURO 6AP’ and ‘EURO6 (AP)’ become ‘EURO 6 AP’; see R/euro_status.R), else variant backfill, else the standard that was compulsory for new registrations of that body type when the vehicle was made; where there is none, why: pre_euro (made before the first standard of its class), no_schedule (body type with no schedule: Other vehicles, or none) or no_date (no date to place the vehicle).

euro_status_src

Source of euro_status: dvla (recorded), variant or model_year_fuel (backfilled from similar vehicles), or euro_schedule (the standard that was compulsory for new registrations of that body type when the vehicle was made).

euro_standard

Euro emission standard number parsed from euro_status (Roman numerals converted).

euro_check

Euro status against the vehicle’s age: ok; later_than_date (the recorded or backfilled status names a standard that did not exist when the vehicle was made - kept, not changed); assumed (filled from the registration-date schedule); pre_euro (made before the first standard of its class); no_schedule (body type with no schedule: Other vehicles, or none); no_date.

euroStatus_DVLA

As returned by the DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service.

realDrivingEmissions_DVLA

As returned by the DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service.
