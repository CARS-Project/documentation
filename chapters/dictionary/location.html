29  Location

29.1 Overview

The only location in the data is the postcode area of the testing station where the vehicle had its MOT test in each year: LS for Leeds, B for Birmingham, and so on. It is where the vehicle was tested, which is usually, but not always, near where it is kept. Chapter 7 explains postcode areas and the limits of analysing vehicles at this scale.

  • pca_YYYY is the postcode area in year YYYY, from 2005 to 2025. Northern Ireland is coded NI.
  • In a year with no recorded postcode area, the pipeline replaces the blank with the vehicle’s status:
    • unborn: the vehicle had not yet been made or registered;
    • dead: the year is after the vehicle’s last MOT test, so it is no longer observed. This is not the same as scrapped: it includes vehicles exported, declared off the road or simply no longer tested;
    • unknown: the vehicle existed but was not observed, for example in its first three years before the first MOT.

The status rules are set out in the methodology, section 3.3, and how the statuses describe a vehicle’s life in Chapter 6. A vehicle is observed in a year when pca_YYYY holds a real postcode area, and selecting these vehicles is the usual first step of any analysis of a year (Chapter 11).

29.2 Coverage

pca_YYYY is never blank, because every blank is replaced by a status, so its coverage is the share of vehicles with a real postcode area rather than a status. Among the vehicles on the road in a year (those that are not unborn or dead), this share is the proportion observed, shown in Figure 29.1.

It is about 88% through the 2010s. The vehicles on the road but not observed are unknown: mostly new vehicles before their first MOT, and vehicles that missed a test. The share falls in the final years, to 76% in 2025: a vehicle last tested in the final two years of data may not yet be due another test, so it stays unknown rather than dead (Chapter 14).

29.3 Values

How the values of each column change over time, for each year. Numeric columns and dates show the minimum, lower quartile (Q1), median, upper quartile (Q3) and maximum; other columns show the share of vehicles with each value, with the less common values grouped as All other values and missing values as Blank.
