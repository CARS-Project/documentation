The RACF / SMMT car fleet, linked to the MOT data

NoteGenerated by the pipeline

This appendix is outputs/reports/racf_linked_report.md from the MOT-pipline repository, copied unchanged into the book by scripts/sync-pipeline.R. Paths such as outputs/... refer to that repository. See Appendix C — Maintaining this book.

Generated from reports/racf_linked_report.qmd by the MOT-pipline racf_linked_report target (profile: main). Tables: outputs/tables/racf/. Edit the template, not this file.

Summary

  • What the RACF data is. DfT’s car fleet (the licensed and SORN cars of VEH0124) by make, model, year of first use and fuel, enriched by RACF with the SMMT segment and with each model’s mass, dimensions, footprint, CO2, energy use and electric range from the EU type-approval and Dutch RDW registers. It holds counts and model attributes only: no vehicle records.
  • It agrees with DfT. The RACF fleet is 99.97%–99.98% of DfT’s VEH0124 car count in every year from 2014 to 2025.
  • Almost every MOT car links. Of the 36,730,468 cars located in 2023, 43.8% link to their exact model string and 55.3% to their generic model (both by year of first use and fuel); 0.9% do not link.
  • But many attributes are proxies. Only 34.4% of the 2023 fleet is matched to the EU data for its exact year; the rest borrows another year’s or fuel’s values. Linked attributes are model averages, not measurements of the vehicle.
  • A defect in the RACF file. 12.3% of the battery electric and fuel cell cars in the 2023 RACF fleet carry an EU CO2 above 0, mostly through RACF’s fuel-agnostic matches, which give an electric model a combustion variant’s figures. The link treats these as missing.
  • What it shows. Between 2014 and 2023 the located car fleet’s SUV share rose from 10.0% to 24.6%, its mean EU mass from 1,375 to 1,415 kg and its mean footprint from 3.97 to 4.03 m², while its mean NEDC CO2 fell from 147 to 133 g/km. SUVs and off-roaders were driven 7,703 miles on average in 2023, against 6,488 for other cars.
  • Validation. The VES CO2 of a car first used up to 2017 is typically 6.7% above its model’s EU NEDC figure, and the VES revenue weight about 1.33 times the EU mass in running order, as a gross weight should be.
  • Cross-checks: 170 of 170 RACF checks pass (quality/cross_checks.csv, areas racf and external).

1. The data and the link

The RACF file (R/racf_fleet.R) has 1.6M rows, one per make, generic model, model string, year of first use, year of manufacture, fuel group and licence status, with DfT’s fleet count in each year (n_2014 to n_2025). RACF matched each row to:

  • the SMMT segment (Mini, Supermini, Lower Medium, …, Dual Purpose, MPV), with crossovers / SUVs (M1) and off-road capable cars (M1G) marked within Dual Purpose;
  • the EU type-approval monitoring data: mass in running order, wheelbase, track, footprint (wheelbase × track), NEDC and WLTP CO2, fuel and energy consumption, electric range;
  • the Dutch RDW register: length, width, height, kerb weight.

Each match has a quality (eu_match_quality, rdw_match_quality): an exact year, the nearest year or another fuel’s values, or a fuzzy name match.

The link (R/racf_linked.R). An MOT car takes the RACF values of rows with the same make, DfT generic model and model string (model), or, failing that, the same make and generic model (genmodel), and in both cases the same year of first use and fuel group. RACF groups non-plug-in hybrids with petrol or diesel, and the pipeline’s plug-in hybrids (fuel_simple, from the DfT fuel detail) with RACF’s PHEV. Attributes are fleet-weighted means over the RACF rows of the key; values outside a plausible range (a height of 15 mm, a wheelbase of 0) are treated as missing.

Which cars. A car (body_type Cars) counts in year Y when it is in a real postcode area that year, as in the DfT comparison (data quality report). This includes the annualisation’s carried-forward years (methodology Section 3.3), so the MOT counts exceed the RACF ones for older cars; see Section 3.

7. Limitations

  • Attributes are model averages. A car takes the fleet-weighted mean of its model, year and fuel, not its own variant’s figure: an estate and a hatchback of one model share a footprint. Means over thousands of cars are sound; a single car’s value is not.
  • Many are borrowed. Only 34.4% of the 2023 fleet has EU figures for its exact year; for the rest RACF used the nearest year or another fuel (Section 1.3). WLTP CO2 covers only models approved since 2017-2018, so the WLTP means describe a newer subset.
  • Cars only. RACF covers DfT cars; vans, motorcycles and buses have no segment or attributes.
  • The MOT stock includes carried-forward years (methodology Section 3.3), so older cars are over-represented against DfT (Section 4.1), and new cars before their first test are missing; fleet means lean slightly towards older models.
  • The link uses the pipeline’s names. A car whose make or model did not match a DfT name links at best to its generic model, or not at all (Section 3).
